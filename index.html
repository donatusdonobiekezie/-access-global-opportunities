Frequently asked questions

Do I need an ACCESS account?

Yes. Users must create an ACCESS account or sign in before using the opportunity experience.

How much can a free member access?

Free members receive access to one verified opportunity. The remaining opportunity library is reserved for Premium members.

Can ACCESS be used globally?

ACCESS is designed for a global audience. Each provider decides where an opportunity is available and who is eligible.

Does ACCESS include AI jobs and AI training work?

Yes. AI-related opportunities are an important category, including AI training, evaluation, data tasks and subject-matter expert work. Availability depends on the provider, location, profile and qualifications.

Will ACCESS guarantee that I get a job or make money?

No. ACCESS helps users discover, understand and organize opportunities. Hiring, acceptance, study invitations, task availability and earnings are controlled by the individual provider.

Does ACCESS bypass country restrictions or verification?

No. ACCESS does not bypass identity checks, country restrictions, eligibility rules, waiting lists, application processes or provider security controls.

Is ACCESS a bank or employer?

No. ACCESS is an opportunity discovery and intelligence platform. It does not guarantee employment, act as a bank, or hold customer funds.
