Frequently asked questions

Can ACCESS be used globally?

ACCESS is designed for a global audience. It can help users discover jobs, side hustles, research studies, freelance opportunities, creator work and AI-related opportunities from different countries. However, each opportunity provider decides where its opportunity is available and who is eligible.

Can ACCESS help me find jobs and side hustles?

Yes. ACCESS is designed to bring legitimate employment, freelance, research, creator, digital-work and side-hustle opportunities into one organized discovery experience, with eligibility and next-step information.

Does ACCESS include AI jobs and AI training work?

Yes. AI-related opportunities are an important category, including AI training, evaluation, data tasks and subject-matter expert work. Platforms such as Outlier publish AI-related expert opportunities, while Prolific offers paid research tasks and also has AI-related studies. Availability depends on the provider and your location, profile and qualifications.

Will ACCESS guarantee that I get a job or make money?

No. ACCESS helps you discover, understand and organize opportunities. Hiring, acceptance, study invitations, task availability and earnings are controlled by the individual opportunity provider.

Does ACCESS bypass country restrictions or verification?

No. ACCESS does not bypass identity checks, country restrictions, eligibility rules, waiting lists, application processes or provider security controls. Users should always provide truthful information and follow the official provider's rules.

How does ACCESS decide whether an opportunity is worth pursuing?

ACCESS can consider factors such as your profile, eligibility, requirements, opportunity quality, freshness and estimated effort to help prioritize what you should review first.

Is ACCESS a bank or employer?

No. ACCESS is an opportunity discovery and intelligence platform. It does not guarantee employment, act as a bank, or hold customer funds.
