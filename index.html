Frequently asked questions

Does ACCESS guarantee income?

No. ACCESS helps users discover and evaluate opportunities. Outcomes depend on eligibility, applications, selection and the individual opportunity provider.

Is ACCESS a bank?

No. ACCESS is an opportunity-discovery and intelligence platform. Any financial account or payment services are provided by regulated third-party providers where available.

Are opportunities verified?

ACCESS is designed to prioritize legitimate sources and verification processes. Users should still review the official opportunity source before applying or paying any fee.

Can people outside Nigeria use ACCESS?

ACCESS is designed with a global audience in mind. Availability and eligibility of individual opportunities and payment services can vary by country.
